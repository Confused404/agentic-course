# Monitor Arm (dual)

SKU HOM-0036 · home-office · $148.99

Gas-spring dual monitor arm, VESA 75/100, up to 9 kg per arm.

## Specifications
- Weight: 4.6 kg
- Materials: Aluminium arm, steel clamp
- In the box: Arm, desk clamp, grommet mount, VESA screws

## Care
Adjust the gas-spring tension with the 4 mm Allen key if the monitor drifts.

## Warranty
5 years on the frame; 1 year on motors and electronics, counted from delivery. See 'Warranty coverage'.

## Questions customers ask
**My monitor drifts down.** Increase the spring tension: turn the hex screw at the elbow towards + until the monitor stays put.

**Will it hold a 34 in ultrawide?** If it weighs 9 kg or less and has VESA 75 or 100 mounts.
