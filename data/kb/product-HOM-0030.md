# Standing Desk (120 cm)

SKU HOM-0030 · home-office · $398.99

Dual-motor electric standing desk, 120 cm top, 4 height presets, 120 kg capacity.

## Specifications
- Weight: 32 kg
- Materials: Steel frame, laminated top
- In the box: Frame, top, control panel, hardware kit, Allen key

## Care
Wipe with a damp cloth. Re-tighten frame bolts after the first month.

## Warranty
5 years on the frame; 1 year on motors and electronics, counted from delivery. See 'Warranty coverage'.

## Questions customers ask
**What's the height range?** 62 to 127 cm.

**The panel shows E2.** E2 means the legs are out of sync. Hold the down button until the desk reaches its lowest point and beeps (reset), then release.
