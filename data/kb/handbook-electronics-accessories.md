# Electronics accessories care handbook

This handbook collects the care, materials and warranty notes for every electronics accessories product family we sell. Product pages have the same notes plus specifications and customer questions.

## Care, materials and what's in the box

**VoltPack Power Bank.** Store at 40–60% charge. Stop using it if it swells: see 'Battery safety for power banks'. Materials: Lithium-polymer cells. In the box: Power bank, USB-C cable.

**Braided USB-C Cable.** None. Materials: Braided nylon jacket, e-marker chip. In the box: Cable.

**GaN Wall Charger.** Keep vents uncovered. Materials: Gallium nitride power stage. In the box: Charger.

**Wireless Charging Pad.** Remove metal cards from the phone case before charging. Materials: Aluminium and silicone. In the box: Pad, USB-C cable (adapter not included).

## Warranty by product family

Counted from delivery; see 'Warranty coverage' for how to claim.

- VoltPack Power Bank: 1 year.
- Braided USB-C Cable: 1 year.
- GaN Wall Charger: 1 year.
- Wireless Charging Pad: 1 year.
