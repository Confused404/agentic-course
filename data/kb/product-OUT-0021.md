# Canister Camp Stove (compact)

SKU OUT-0021 · outdoor · $38.99

Ultralight canister stove, compact. Boils 1 L in about 4 minutes. Fuel sold separately.

## Specifications
- Weight: 85 g
- Materials: Aluminium and titanium burner
- In the box: Stove, hard case

## Care
Let it cool before packing. Keep the jet clean; don't use it inside a closed tent.

## Warranty
2 years, excluding wear and tear, counted from delivery. See 'Warranty coverage'.

## Questions customers ask
**Which fuel does it use?** Standard threaded isobutane-propane canisters (EN 417). Fuel is sold separately and can't be shipped by air.

**The piezo igniter stopped sparking.** Check the electrode gap is 4 mm; light with a match meanwhile. Igniters are covered for 2 years.
