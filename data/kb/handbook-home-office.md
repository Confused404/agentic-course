# Home office care handbook

This handbook collects the care, materials and warranty notes for every home office product family we sell. Product pages have the same notes plus specifications and customer questions.

## Care, materials and what's in the box

**Standing Desk.** Wipe with a damp cloth. Re-tighten frame bolts after the first month. Materials: Steel frame, laminated top. In the box: Frame, top, control panel, hardware kit, Allen key.

**Ergonomic Chair.** Vacuum fabric; wipe mesh with a damp cloth. Materials: Aluminium base, class-4 gas lift. In the box: Chair (seat, back, base), casters, hardware.

**Monitor Arm.** Adjust the gas-spring tension with the 4 mm Allen key if the monitor drifts. Materials: Aluminium arm, steel clamp. In the box: Arm, desk clamp, grommet mount, VESA screws.

**Under-Desk Cable Tray.** None. Materials: Powder-coated steel. In the box: Tray, 2 clamps.

## Warranty by product family

Counted from delivery; see 'Warranty coverage' for how to claim.

- Standing Desk: 5 years on the frame; 1 year on motors and electronics.
- Ergonomic Chair: 5 years on the frame; 1 year on motors and electronics.
- Monitor Arm: 5 years on the frame; 1 year on motors and electronics.
- Under-Desk Cable Tray: 5 years on the frame; 1 year on motors and electronics.
