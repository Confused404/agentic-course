# Squeeze Water Filter (standard)

SKU OUT-0028 · outdoor · $28.99

Hollow-fibre water filter, standard, removes 99.9999% of bacteria.

## Specifications
- Weight: 85 g
- Materials: Hollow-fibre membrane (0.1 micron)
- In the box: Filter, backflush syringe

## Care
Backflush after every trip. Never let it freeze when wet: ice cracks the fibres invisibly; replace a filter that may have frozen.

## Warranty
2 years, excluding wear and tear, counted from delivery. See 'Warranty coverage'.

## Questions customers ask
**Does it remove viruses?** No. It removes bacteria and protozoa; boil or treat chemically where viruses are a concern.
