# Kitchen care handbook

This handbook collects the care, materials and warranty notes for every kitchen product family we sell. Product pages have the same notes plus specifications and customer questions.

## Care, materials and what's in the box

**Cast Iron Skillet.** Hand-wash, dry immediately, wipe with a thin layer of oil. Never soak or put in the dishwasher. Materials: Cast iron, pre-seasoned with vegetable oil. In the box: Skillet, silicone handle sleeve.

**Enameled Dutch Oven.** Dishwasher-safe, but hand-washing keeps the enamel glossy. Avoid metal utensils and sudden temperature changes (thermal shock can crack enamel). Materials: Enameled cast iron; stainless steel lid knob. In the box: Pot, lid.

**Chef's Knife.** Hand-wash only and dry immediately. Hone weekly; sharpen on a whetstone at 15° per side. Materials: High-carbon stainless steel (58 HRC), full tang, G10 handle. In the box: Knife, blade guard.

**End-Grain Cutting Board.** Oil monthly with food-safe mineral oil. Hand-wash; never soak. Small surface cracks can be sanded and re-oiled. Materials: End-grain hardwood, food-safe mineral oil finish. In the box: Board, rubber feet (pre-fitted).

**Gooseneck Pour-Over Kettle.** Descale monthly with a 1:1 water and white vinegar solution. Never immerse the base. Materials: Stainless steel body, BPA-free base. In the box: Kettle, base, quick-start card.

**French Press.** Dishwasher-safe. Unscrew the filter stack monthly to clean the screens. Materials: Double-wall 18/8 stainless steel. In the box: Press, spare filter screen.
