# Outdoor gear care handbook

This handbook collects the care, materials and warranty notes for every outdoor gear product family we sell. Product pages have the same notes plus specifications and customer questions.

## Care, materials and what's in the box

**Trailhead Tent.** Never store wet: dry fully within 24 hours. Re-proof the fly yearly. See 'Tent care and waterproofing'. Materials: 20D ripstop nylon fly with DWR coating, 3,000 mm hydrostatic head; aluminium poles. In the box: Tent body, fly, poles, 10 stakes, guylines, stuff sack, repair sleeve.

**Summit Backpack.** Hand-wash with mild soap; air dry. Don't machine-wash (it damages the coating). Materials: 210D nylon with PU coating; aluminium frame stay. In the box: Pack, rain cover.

**Daypack.** Hand-wash; air dry. Materials: 30D silnylon. In the box: Daypack.

**Canister Camp Stove.** Let it cool before packing. Keep the jet clean; don't use it inside a closed tent. Materials: Aluminium and titanium burner. In the box: Stove, hard case.

**Down Sleeping Bag.** Store uncompressed in the storage sack. Wash rarely, with down wash, and tumble-dry low with dryer balls. Materials: 650-fill responsibly sourced down, 20D nylon shell. In the box: Bag, compression sack, storage sack.

**Headlamp.** Charge every 3 months in storage. IPX4: splash-proof, not submersible. Materials: Polycarbonate housing; 1,200 mAh Li-ion battery. In the box: Headlamp, USB-C cable.

**Squeeze Water Filter.** Backflush after every trip. Never let it freeze when wet: ice cracks the fibres invisibly; replace a filter that may have frozen. Materials: Hollow-fibre membrane (0.1 micron). In the box: Filter, backflush syringe.
