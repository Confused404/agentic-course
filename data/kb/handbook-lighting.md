# Lighting care handbook

This handbook collects the care, materials and warranty notes for every lighting product family we sell. Product pages have the same notes plus specifications and customer questions.

## Care, materials and what's in the box

**Dimmable LED Bulb A19.** Not for enclosed fixtures. Materials: Polycarbonate diffuser. In the box: Bulb.

**Smart Bulb.** Not for enclosed fixtures or dimmer circuits. Materials: Polycarbonate diffuser. In the box: Bulb, quick-start card.

**Arc Floor Lamp.** Dust the shade with a soft brush. Materials: Steel arc, marble base, linen shade. In the box: Base, arc in 3 sections, shade, E27 socket.

**Outdoor String Lights.** IP65: rain-proof; don't submerge the plug. Materials: Shatterproof PC bulbs, IP65 cable. In the box: Lights, 2 spare bulbs, mounting clips.

## Warranty by product family

Counted from delivery; see 'Warranty coverage' for how to claim.

- Dimmable LED Bulb A19: 2 years.
- Smart Bulb: 2 years.
- Arc Floor Lamp: 2 years.
- Outdoor String Lights: 2 years.
