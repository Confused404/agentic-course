End-grain maple cutting board, gentle on knife edges. Oil monthly with food-safe mineral oil.

## Specifications

- Materials: End-grain hardwood, food-safe mineral oil finish
- In the box: Board, rubber feet (pre-fitted)

## Care
Oil monthly with food-safe mineral oil. Hand-wash; never soak. Small surface cracks can be sanded and re-oiled.

## Warranty
1 year, counted from delivery. See 'Warranty coverage'.

## Questions customers ask
**Why end grain?** The knife edge slips between wood fibres instead of cutting across them, so edges stay sharp longer and cuts close up.

**Does it come pre-oiled?** Yes, but oil it again before first use.
