## Questions customers ask
**The display shows E1. What does that mean?** E1 means the kettle was switched on empty or ran dry. Let it cool for 10 minutes, fill it above the MIN line, and switch on again.

**How long does hold mode keep the temperature?** Up to 60 minutes.
