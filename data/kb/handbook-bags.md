# Bags care handbook

This handbook collects the care, materials and warranty notes for every bags product family we sell. Product pages have the same notes plus specifications and customer questions.

## Care, materials and what's in the box

**Commuter Backpack.** Spot-clean only. Materials: Recycled 600D polyester with TPU coating. In the box: Backpack.

**Duffel Bag.** Machine-wash cold, gentle cycle; air dry. Materials: Recycled nylon. In the box: Duffel, shoulder strap.

**Packing Cubes.** Machine-wash cold. Materials: Ripstop nylon, mesh tops. In the box: Cubes.

## Warranty by product family

Counted from delivery; see 'Warranty coverage' for how to claim.

- Commuter Backpack: 1 year.
- Duffel Bag: 1 year.
- Packing Cubes: 1 year.
